# Appendix K: Complete Worked Examples

## Why It Matters
Worked examples show whole-system assembly: files, scripts, config, cadence, and output. They are the closest thing to end-to-end reference implementations.

## Core Ideas
- Each example combines multiple chapter patterns into a coherent workflow.
- Best used for imitation, benchmarking, and training, not blind copying.
- They reveal which artifacts are actually needed in practice.

## Patterns To Keep
- Use worked examples as rehearsal for internal stack design.
- Extract shared scaffolds instead of cloning entire examples.

## What To Adopt In OpenClaw
- Turn the strongest examples into internal demo projects or starter packs.
- Use them to validate whether our own docs are concrete enough.

## Risks / Limits
- Examples can import hidden assumptions about environment and operator habits.
- Full examples are heavy; teams often need a thinner path.
