# Appendix L: Rapid Reference — The 50 Most Useful

## Why It Matters
This is the pocket reference. It reduces context-switch cost during real operation.

## Core Ideas
- Keep the most-used commands and patterns close.
- Good rapid references favor action over explanation.

## Patterns To Keep
- Distill repeated operational moves into a short cheat sheet.
- Prefer command clusters: gateway, files, cron, browser, debugging.

## What To Adopt In OpenClaw
- Create a repo-local quick-reference once the team’s actual top commands are known.
- Keep it short enough to scan under pressure.

## Risks / Limits
- Quick references go stale fast.
- They can hide the nuance needed for risky operations.
