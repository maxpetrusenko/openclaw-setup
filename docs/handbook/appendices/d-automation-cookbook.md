# Appendix D: Automation Cookbook

## Why It Matters
This appendix is recipe-first. It is where patterns become runnable compositions.

## Core Ideas
- Cookbook entries compress setup, recipe, and variations into copyable units.
- Repeated patterns show up here with less theory and more wiring.
- Useful as seed material for internal templates or starter automations.

## Patterns To Keep
- Treat recipes as starting points, not drop-in truth.
- Preserve the problem, ingredients, recipe, variation structure.
- Pull out reusable scaffolds when multiple recipes share shape.

## What To Adopt In OpenClaw
- Mine cookbook recipes for starter automations and demo flows.
- Convert the best ones into internal templates or skills.

## Risks / Limits
- Recipes may encode assumptions about paths, tools, or models that are no longer current.
- Copy-paste without adaptation creates brittle automation.
