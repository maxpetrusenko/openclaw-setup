# Appendix F: The Practitioner's Field Guide

## Why It Matters
This appendix is the “I am building right now” companion. It answers implementation-time questions with concrete moves instead of theory.

## Core Ideas
- Most paralysis comes from picking the wrong first automation or from weak scoping.
- Practical guidance beats abstract architecture when a system is still being formed.
- The appendix is organized around friction, not concepts.

## Patterns To Keep
- Use it during execution, not just during planning.
- Translate repeated advice into checklists or prompts.
- Let real operator questions shape follow-up docs.

## What To Adopt In OpenClaw
- Mine this appendix for onboarding checklists and operator prompts.
- Use it as a source for “what to do when stuck” runbooks.

## Risks / Limits
- Advice is contextual and may assume handbook defaults that do not match this repo.
- Easy to treat heuristics as hard law.

## Open Questions
